# Kabigon loader parity

Audit date: 2026-09-22

This matrix compares `@narumitw/sumire-url-content` with Kabigon at commit [`b68986bd4bf76f89aeacde31ad278e1d067ddb65`](https://github.com/narumiruna/kabigon/tree/b68986bd4bf76f89aeacde31ad278e1d067ddb65) (`0.19.6` baseline). The authoritative registries are [Sumire's `loader-registry.ts`](../packages/url-content/src/loader-registry.ts) and [Kabigon's pinned `loader_registry.py`](https://github.com/narumiruna/kabigon/blob/b68986bd4bf76f89aeacde31ad278e1d067ddb65/src/kabigon/loader_registry.py).

All 20 Kabigon loader IDs exist in Sumire. Sumire additionally registers `anydoc`, `threads`, and `google-docs`. Kabigon's `loaders/audio.py` supports transcription but is not a registered loader.

## Reading the matrix

- **S/K kind** is the Sumire/Kabigon admission class: request, browser, or worker.
- **Requirements** distinguishes registry requirements from runtime dependencies. `FIRECRAWL_API_KEY` is the only registry requirement in either implementation.
- Limits and cancellation describe Sumire. A reusable `UrlContentClient` also applies its total deadline while waiting for admission, validates public HTTP(S) targets and redirects, and preserves capacity until admitted work settles.
- Every loader returns a string. Sumire's load chain adds `loaderId`, `contentType`, downgrade state, ordered attempts, and typed errors around that string.

## Compatibility limits and audit outcome

Name parity does not mean byte-for-byte output parity. The audit found no unintentional behavior gap that should be copied from Kabigon. The observed differences are retained intentionally:

1. Sumire narrows PTT and LTN automatic applicability to article URLs, verifies requested social-post IDs, and rejects broad BBC/CNN `<main>` shells. These checks prevent a strict source pipeline from returning a listing, login page, or unrelated shell.
2. Sumire enforces public-target and redirect validation, response/media limits, bounded browser defaults, caller cancellation, context/process cleanup, and worker admission where Kabigon relies more heavily on its shared deadline or blocking runner.
3. JavaScript runtime substitutions can change Markdown whitespace or transcript availability: `fetch` replaces `httpx`, `impers` replaces `curl_cffi`, `youtube-transcript` replaces `youtube-transcript-api`, and command-line Whisper replaces the embedded Python model.
4. Sumire's extra `anydoc`, `threads`, and `google-docs` loaders remain outside the Kabigon baseline set.

No live source smoke was needed: URL routing, selectors, fallback decisions, limits, cancellation, cleanup, and output contracts are covered by deterministic fixtures. The relevant evidence is in [`applicability.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/applicability.test.ts), [`extractors.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/extractors.test.ts), [`loaders.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/loaders.test.ts), [`browser.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/browser.test.ts), [`youtube.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/youtube.test.ts), [`ytdlp.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/ytdlp.test.ts), and [`loader-registry-parity.test.ts`](../packages/url-content/tests/loader-registry-parity.test.ts).
